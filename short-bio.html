Short Bio

Octavio Perez-Kempner is a Research Engineer at NTT Social Informatics Laboratories in Tokyo, where he works on trustworthy digital identity and privacy-enhancing technologies. His research develops cryptographic foundations and protocols for secure and privacy-preserving digital identity systems, with particular interests in anonymous credentials, device-bound credentials, privacy-preserving authentication and single sign-on, digital wallets, advanced signature schemes, and post-quantum cryptography.

His work spans foundational cryptographic design and practical protocol considerations, with the goal of connecting new cryptographic techniques to emerging identity architectures, standards, and deployments. At NTT, he contributes core research supporting digital identity initiatives across the broader NTT ecosystem.

He received his Ph.D. from École Normale Supérieure – Université PSL, where his doctoral research focused on malleable cryptography, zero-knowledge proofs, and privacy-preserving authentication. His research has appeared at venues including IEEE Symposium on Security and Privacy, ASIACRYPT, PKC, ESORICS, and Financial Cryptography.
